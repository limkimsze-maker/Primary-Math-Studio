Teacher guide

P1 · 2D shapes

Name and describe rectangles, squares, triangles, circles, half circles and quarter circles. Explicitly teach vertex for one corner and vertices for more than one. Pupils count straight sides and vertices and recognise component shapes in simple composite figures.

P2 · Patterns and 3D shapes

Complete patterns involving shape, size, colour and orientation. Identify, name, describe and classify cubes, cuboids, cones, cylinders and spheres.

P3 · Angles and lines

Recognise right angles and compare other angles with a right angle. Recognise and draw parallel and perpendicular lines.

Sorter approach: inspired by your P3 Angle Dock and Line Dock activities. Pupils classify the visual first; no degree or answer label is printed on the object. Angle sizes and line orientations vary so pupils do not rely on position.
